Title:
lightWorks

Subtitle:
Comprehensive Lighting & Optical Effects Framework

Description:
lightWorks is the theWorks ecosystem framework for light sources, illumination, optical effects, modular bulbs, emission spectra, sunlight, and UV exposure. Its current release turns compatible portable lights into serviceable equipment with replaceable bulbs, maintenance tools, burnout, colored visible light, tactical visibility profiles, attachment support, and reusable exposure APIs.

It supports verified flashlights, angle-headed lights, and weapon lights while preserving their original item identity. Other mods can register exact compatible lights and custom emission channels. Project Nightfall uses lightWorks for UV sources and natural/artificial exposure, then owns all infected-specific consequences. Required powerWorks integration supplies sized batteries and consumption accounting.

Automatic discovery reports probable modded lights for human review. A discovered item is never made serviceable until an exact compatibility provider verifies it.

Features:
- Transactional install, remove, and change-bulb actions.
- Nil-safe inventory context-menu integration.
- White, red, colored, UV, and extensible custom bulbs.
- Colored emitted light without tinting the item icon or world model.
- Per-device beam range, strength, width, node count, and falloff profiles.
- Persistent bulb state, tool requirements, timed servicing, and burnout.
- Belt/webbing attachment profiles and weapon-light support.
- Context-aware tactical spawn distributions.
- Public compatibility, visibility, and emission APIs.

Requirements:
- Project Zomboid Build 42.20.3 or later
- powerWorks

Known limitations:
- Automatically discovered lights require a reviewed compatibility patch.
- Multiplayer compatibility remains under active testing.
