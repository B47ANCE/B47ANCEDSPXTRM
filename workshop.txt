description=[h1]B47ANCED SPXTRM[/h1]
description=
description=Lighting, optics, sunlight, ultraviolet exposure, and serviceable-light infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.
description=
description=B47ANCED SPXTRM owns bulbs, compatible emitters, rendered light behavior, and reusable lighting semantics for players, Works modules, and compatible third-party mods.
description=
description=[h2]Current Features[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Replaceable bulbs for registered flashlights, angle-headed lights, weapon lights, and compatible world fixtures
description=[*]White, warm incandescent, cool LED, red, green, colored, and extensible emission profiles
description=[*]Correct colored portable beams with standardized visible blue UV
description=[*]Separate visible color, detection behavior, and non-visible semantic emissions
description=[*]Device-specific beam geometry, range, strength, direction, and falloff
description=[*]Profiles for penlights, standard and Heavy-Duty flashlights, weapon lights, angle-headed lights, lanterns, and other registered emitters
description=[*]Tool requirements, timed servicing, persistent bulb state, and bulb burnout
description=[*]Device-owned context menus with bulb icons and installed-bulb status
description=[*]Registered bulbs for supported vanilla lamps and bulb-receiving fixtures
description=[*]Tactical spawn distributions, including 80% red and 20% white Army angle-headed flashlights
description=[*]Realistic belt, webbing, attached, and weapon-mounted light profiles
description=[*]Downward belt halos for straight flashlights and side-facing cones for angle-headed lights
description=[*]Heavy-Duty flashlight attachment restrictions based on weight and practical use
description=[*]Automatic modded-light discovery with a bounded human-verification queue
description=[*]Verified compatibility-provider API for third-party flashlight patches
description=[*]Public APIs for bulbs, compatible lights, spectra, emitters, sunlight, and UV exposure
description=[*]Persistent dropped-light direction with server-authoritative cardinal aiming, visible cone chains, wall occlusion, and matching UV semantics
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Lighting Patterns & Battery Behavior[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Device-owned selectors for steady, strobe, pulse, beacon, SOS, alternating, and emergency patterns
description=[*]Persistent server-authoritative pattern changes with stale and unknown request rejection
description=[*]Average duty-cycle power accounting through B47ANCEDPower
description=[*]Deterministic near-empty battery flicker for handheld, attached, and weapon-mounted lights
description=[*]Configurable flicker threshold, cadence, and near-empty off-window strength
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sunlight & Exposure[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Persistent authority-owned natural sunlight dose and sunburn severity
description=[*]Bounded sun-protection provider API
description=[*]Replay-safe treatment integration for B47ANCED Health Care
description=[*]Sandbox controls for enablement, onset, maximum dose, exposure rate, and sheltered recovery
description=[*]Bounded player and world-emitter collection with independent contributions from overlapping UV sources
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires B47ANCEDPower for battery size, persistent charge, servicing, consumption, pattern drain, and low-power flicker
description=[*]Owns artificial-UV and sunlight semantics consumed by Project Nightfall
description=[*]Exposes sunlight and sunburn state to B47ANCED Health Care treatment providers
description=[*]Supports compatible vanilla and third-party lights through verified providers
description=[*]Does not require Project Nightfall and does not own Nightfall infected damage or behavior
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Lighting Semantics[/h2]
description=
description=Visible color, detection behavior, and semantic emissions are separate properties. Other mods can use red tactical-light behavior, ultraviolet output, or custom spectra without duplicating flashlight compatibility and rendering rules.

description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=
description=Unknown modded lights are inspected safely and placed in a review queue; they are not silently treated as verified. Compatibility patches register approved lights once, after which servicing, bulb color, UV semantics, rendering, and B47ANCEDPower behavior flow through the canonical registries.
description=
