[h1]B47ANCED Spectrum[/h1]

Lighting, optics, sunlight, ultraviolet exposure, and serviceable-light infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

B47ANCED Spectrum owns bulbs, compatible emitters, rendered light behavior, and reusable lighting semantics for players, Works modules, and compatible third-party mods.

[h2]Current Features[/h2]

[list]
[*]Replaceable bulbs for registered flashlights, angle-headed lights, weapon lights, and compatible world fixtures
[*]White, warm incandescent, cool LED, red, green, colored, and extensible emission profiles
[*]Correct colored portable beams with standardized visible blue UV
[*]Separate visible color, detection behavior, and non-visible semantic emissions
[*]Device-specific beam geometry, range, strength, direction, and falloff
[*]Profiles for penlights, standard and Heavy-Duty flashlights, weapon lights, angle-headed lights, lanterns, and other registered emitters
[*]Tool requirements, timed servicing, persistent bulb state, and bulb burnout
[*]Device-owned context menus with bulb icons and installed-bulb status
[*]Registered bulbs for supported vanilla lamps and bulb-receiving fixtures
[*]Tactical spawn distributions, including 80% red and 20% white Army angle-headed flashlights
[*]Realistic belt, webbing, attached, and weapon-mounted light profiles
[*]Downward belt halos for straight flashlights and side-facing cones for angle-headed lights
[*]Heavy-Duty flashlight attachment restrictions based on weight and practical use
[*]Automatic modded-light discovery with a bounded human-verification queue
[*]Verified compatibility-provider API for third-party flashlight patches
[*]Public APIs for bulbs, compatible lights, spectra, emitters, sunlight, and UV exposure
[*]Persistent dropped-light direction with server-authoritative cardinal aiming, visible cone chains, wall occlusion, and matching UV semantics
[/list]

[h2]Lighting Patterns & Battery Behavior[/h2]

[list]
[*]Device-owned selectors for steady, strobe, pulse, beacon, SOS, alternating, and emergency patterns
[*]Persistent server-authoritative pattern changes with stale and unknown request rejection
[*]Average duty-cycle power accounting through B47ANCEDPower
[*]Deterministic near-empty battery flicker for handheld, attached, and weapon-mounted lights
[*]Configurable flicker threshold, cadence, and near-empty off-window strength
[/list]

[h2]Sunlight & Exposure[/h2]

[list]
[*]Persistent authority-owned natural sunlight dose and sunburn severity
[*]Bounded sun-protection provider API
[*]Replay-safe treatment integration for medWorks
[*]Sandbox controls for enablement, onset, maximum dose, exposure rate, and sheltered recovery
[*]Bounded player and world-emitter collection with independent contributions from overlapping UV sources
[/list]

[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]

[list]
[*]Requires B47ANCEDPower for battery size, persistent charge, servicing, consumption, pattern drain, and low-power flicker
[*]Owns artificial-UV and sunlight semantics consumed by Project Nightfall
[*]Exposes sunlight and sunburn state to medWorks treatment providers
[*]Supports compatible vanilla and third-party lights through verified providers
[*]Does not require Project Nightfall and does not own Nightfall infected damage or behavior
[/list]

[h2]Lighting Semantics[/h2]

Visible color, detection behavior, and semantic emissions are separate properties. Other mods can use red tactical-light behavior, ultraviolet output, or custom spectra without duplicating flashlight compatibility and rendering rules.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Unknown modded lights are inspected safely and placed in a review queue; they are not silently treated as verified. Compatibility patches register approved lights once, after which servicing, bulb color, UV semantics, rendering, and B47ANCEDPower behavior flow through the canonical registries.

[i]Dedicated-server multiplayer compatibility has not yet been certified.[/i]

[h2]Development feature additions[/h2]
Free-angle world-light geometry, mounted aiming-laser presentation, registered
advanced fixture adapters, local sunburn status and clothing protection, and
conservative legacy migration are implemented in source with offline regressions.
Update the required B47ANCEDPower dependency and all clients/server together.
Numeric aiming angles are not supported by older Spectrum clients. Unknown legacy
records and unresolved bulb identities remain quarantined. Native presentation,
save/streaming and dedicated behavior remain NOT TESTED; G7 and stable G8 BLOCKED.

[h2]Optional consumer integration APIs[/h2]
Spectrum declares two Nightfall stored-bulb aliases, delegates the existing
Medical sunburn procedure, registers opt-in UVC Water treatment and reports crop
illumination without changing growth. Water integration defaults off; ordinary
Nightfall bulbs are UVA. No UVC device or treatment action is supplied.
Refreshed Nightfall metadata matches B47ANCEDHealthCare. Source preflights pass,
without certifying native loading, gameplay or installation.

[h2]Bounded field observations[/h2]
Clustered exposure and paged read-only crop-light APIs reuse one bounded source
scan for up to 32 nearby targets. No growth effect or cached treatment evidence.
Three audit fixes harden prototype debug gating, fixture registration and laser
geometry. MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED.
